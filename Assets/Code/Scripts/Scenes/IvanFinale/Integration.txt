IVAN FINALE — integration and preview

Scene: Assets/Level/Scenes/Locations/laba/FinalScene.unity
The finale starts after the laboratory investigation and Ivan's existing
dialogue. Alexei restores reserve power, isolates the compartment and opens
the father's door. The existing father cutscene hands the black screen to
IvanFinaleController on completion. Ivan starts seated at the control desk,
then rises and receives first-person controls.

For a cutscene-only test, enable Skip Gameplay For Testing on
FinalLabFlowController. The normal scene leaves the skip disabled.

Optional, before BeginIvanPhase():
  UseAlexeiBody(fallenVisualBody)
  UseFatherBody(fatherVisualBody)
Use independent visual objects, never the playable FirstPersonPlayer hierarchy.
The supplied scene already contains fallback bodies. The finale changes their
pose, visibility and position. Keep the supplied rescue positions when possible:
the CCTV and extraction beam are composed for this containment room.

To preview independently: open the scene, enter Play Mode, then choose
Tools > Final Lab > Start Ivan phase in Play Mode.
Tools > Final Lab > Install Ivan finale rebuilds the generated rig; it is a
development tool and will replace edits inside that rig. It also imports the
existing Metro scene's stationary train and platform, without their gameplay.

Controls: WASD/mouse; aim at a mechanism and press E. Hold E at the resonance
lever until isolation reaches 100%. The father's schematic shows ON/OFF/ON.
Release the mains latch before lowering the breaker; open the destruction
cover before pressing the overload button. The 60-second deadline includes
lifting Alexei. R retries the escape checkpoint after a timeout.
Reach the last-carriage exit with Alexei, walk through the open train door,
then press E at the platform marker. The final conversation plays in subtitles.

Completion: use the serialized onCompleted UnityEvent or the Completed event
to trigger credits or the next scene. State remains Complete afterwards.

Audio slots, assign with SetVoiceClip(index, clip) or in spokenLines:
0 carrier warning; 1 mains disconnected; 2 entity using father's voice;
3 Ivan's refusal; 4 Ivan's Mode B line; 5 overload warning; 6 apology;
7–11 final conversation in order; 12 train arrival/destruction.
Only slot 2 currently has a temporary locally synthesized Russian voice.
It uses pitch, low-pass and echo processing. Replace it with the actor recording.
Other dialogue currently uses subtitles. Mechanism and strain sounds are
generated by the controller, without external audio dependencies.

The platform is reused from the project's Metro scene with a dawn light.
The carry and departure poses are procedural; this is not motion capture.
